# Campus Connect

Campus Connect is the shared language for an unofficial campus activity application for students and teachers at IU Campus Bad Honnef.

## People and groups

**User**:
A person with a Campus Connect account. A user may discover activities, create activities, or do both.
_Avoid_: Account holder

**Creator**:
A user who publishes a personal event or manages content for a group. Creator describes how a user is acting, not a permanent role.
_Avoid_: Organiser account

**Explorer**:
A user who discovers groups, posts, or events and may follow or participate. Explorer describes how a user is acting, not a permanent role.
_Avoid_: Attendee account

**Group**:
A named campus community that publishes posts and group events through its members.
_Avoid_: Club, channel

**Group membership**:
The relationship between a user and a group, with exactly one of the roles owner, representative, or subscriber.
_Avoid_: Group user

**Owner**:
The group member with primary responsibility for the group and authority to appoint representatives.
_Avoid_: Admin

**Representative**:
A group member appointed by the owner to manage the group's posts and events.
_Avoid_: Moderator

**Subscriber**:
A group member who follows the group's posts and events without managing them.
_Avoid_: Follower

**Campus Updates**:
The campus-wide group whose content is available to every user.
_Avoid_: Priority group, global group

## Content and participation

**Post**:
A feed item published by a group. A post may be a standalone update or the feed representation of an event.
_Avoid_: Announcement

**Event**:
A scheduled campus activity published by a user or group and represented in the feed by one post.
_Avoid_: Activity, meeting

**Personal event**:
An event published by a user independently of a group.

**Group event**:
An event published on behalf of a group by its owner or a representative.

**Response mode**:
The participation rule for an event: announcement, interest, or registration.
_Avoid_: RSVP type

**Announcement**:
A response mode that presents event information without collecting participation.

**Interest**:
A response mode that records a user's non-binding interest in an event.
_Avoid_: Like

**Registration**:
A response mode that records attendance against an event's available capacity.
_Avoid_: Booking

**Participation**:
A user's recorded interest or registration for an event. One user may have at most one participation record per event.
_Avoid_: RSVP

## Access and discovery

**Visibility**:
The audience allowed to discover and view an event: public, subscribers, or invitees.
_Avoid_: Privacy level

**Public event**:
An event visible to users without a relationship to its creator or group.

**Subscriber-only event**:
A group event visible only to members of its group.
_Avoid_: Followers-only event

**Invite-only event**:
An event visible only to invited users or people using an active share link.
_Avoid_: Private event

**Invitation**:
Access granted to a named user for an invite-only event.

**Share link**:
A revocable link that grants access to an invite-only event.
_Avoid_: Public link

**Tag**:
A reusable hobby or interest label attached to events for discovery and filtering.
_Avoid_: Category

**Location**:
A named place with coordinates where an event takes place.
_Avoid_: Venue

**Notification**:
A stored in-application update about followed-group content or a relevant event change.
_Avoid_: Alert, push notification
